02 — Requirements & verification

What you need to ride.

We verify every rider properly — it protects customers, and it protects you. Have clear photos of these ready before you apply; you upload them straight into the form.

  • Your NIN slip or NIN card11-digit number plus a photo of the document. We verify it against the national database.
  • Driver's licence or dispatch rider's cardValid and unexpired. We match the name and photo to your NIN and selfie.
  • A clear selfieTaken now, good light, no cap or sunglasses — used for a face match against your ID.
  • Bike papersIf you own your bike: proof of ownership / registration. On Pay As You Ride, we handle the papers.
  • 2 guarantors — with NIN and addressEach guarantor gives their full name, phone, home address, relationship to you, NIN number and a photo of their NIN. We call both.
  • 18+ and a smartphone with WhatsAppYou'll receive dispatches and share your live location on every trip.

How we keep it serious

Every document you upload goes to a private, access-controlled folder — only Ngwanu Express onboarding staff can open it. We confirm your NIN and licence against official records, run a face match against your selfie, call both guarantors, and check for any history of theft or fraud. Giving false information or a fake document ends the application and is reported to the authorities.

Pay As You Ride — no bike? No problem.

Select "I need a bike (Pay As You Ride)" on the form. We assign you a bike and set up easy weekly payments deducted from your earnings until it's fully yours. The same ID and guarantor checks apply.
