02 — Requirements

What you need to ride.

We verify every rider properly — it protects customers and it protects you. Have these ready before you apply.

  • A bike — yours or on Pay As You RideOwn a bike with valid papers, or apply for one of ours on weekly instalments.
  • Valid NIN and Driver's LicenseOriginal documents — we confirm the details and match them to your selfie.
  • Smartphone with WhatsAppYou'll receive dispatches and share your live location from it.
  • 2 guarantors with IDEach provides their own valid ID and agrees to a short verification call.
  • 18 years or olderAnd familiar with the roads across your delivery zone.

How verification works

Fill the short form on this page. Our onboarding team then calls or WhatsApps you to collect clear photos of your NIN/ID, a selfie, your bike papers, and your two guarantors' IDs. Verification usually takes under 3 days.

Pay As You Ride — no bike? No problem.

Select "I need a bike (Pay As You Ride)" on the form. We'll assign you a bike and set up easy weekly payments deducted from your earnings until it's fully yours. Guarantor checks still apply.
