Briefing

A chain of energy orbs is crawling toward the BREACH. Stop it before it gets there.

  • AIM & FIRE — move the mouse and click, or tap anywhere on a phone.
  • SWAP — right-click, press Space, tap the panther, or use the Swap button.
  • Line up 3 or more of the same colour to destroy them.
  • When a gap closes on matching colours the chain snaps back — that's a COMBO.
  • Consecutive matching shots build a STREAK bonus.
  • Destroy glowing orbs for power-ups: BOMB, SLOW-MO, REVERSE, LASER SIGHT.
  • Finish with the chain far from the breach for an INTEGRITY bonus. Extra life every 30,000 points.
