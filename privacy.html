Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Ngwanu Express ("we", "us") runs a motorbike delivery service in Lagos, Nigeria, plus a rider recruitment programme. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect through ngwanuexpress.github.io and its booking, tracking and rider-application forms, why we collect it, and how it's kept safe.

1. Information we collect

If you book a delivery

Your name, phone number, the pickup and drop-off addresses (plus their map coordinates if you pick a suggested address or use "Use my current location"), the item type, and the estimated fare.

If you apply to ride with us

Your full name, phone number, date of birth, home address, NIN, the area you'll cover, and your bike details. We also collect, as photos or PDFs: your NIN slip, your driver's licence or rider's card, a selfie, and — if you own a bike — your bike papers.

For each of your two guarantors: their name, phone, home address, relationship to you, NIN, and a photo of their NIN.

We do not collect passwords, bank details, or card numbers anywhere on this site.

2. Why we collect it

  • Bookings — to match you with a nearby rider, confirm the price by phone, and share a live tracking link.
  • Rider identity documents — to verify who you are, confirm your NIN and licence against official records, match your face to your ID, and run a background check before you're allowed to carry customers' packages.
  • Guarantor details — we call both guarantors to confirm they know you and agree to stand for you, as part of protecting customers from fraud or theft.

Giving false information or an altered/forged document ends an application immediately and may be reported to the police.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking and rider-application records for as long as reasonably needed for dispatch, dispute resolution, and (for riders) fraud and safety checks — and to meet any legal record-keeping obligation. If your rider application isn't approved, or you ask us to, we'll delete your documents on request (see below).

5. Your rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that's wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information — including uploaded ID documents — where we're not required to keep it.
  • Withdraw a rider application at any time before onboarding.

To do any of these, just email or WhatsApp us (details below) — we'll action it as quickly as we can.

6. Keeping it safe

Uploaded documents (NIN, licence, selfie, bike papers, guarantor IDs) are saved to a private Google Drive folder that is not publicly listed or shared — only Ngwanu Express's own Google account can open it. We don't sell, rent, or use your information for advertising.

7. Age

Our rider programme is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If how we handle information changes, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
