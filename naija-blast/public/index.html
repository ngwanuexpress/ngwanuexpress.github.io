    How to play

    Everything you need, in one screen.

    GoalOrbs roll toward the breach. Clear them before one falls in.
    MouseAim with the pointer and click to fire. Right-click or Space swaps your two orbs.
    TouchDrag to aim, release to fire. Tap the leopard or the Swap button to swap.
    MatchLine up three or more of one colour. The ring shows where your orb will land.
    ComboWhen a gap closes on matching colours the chain snaps back and bursts again. Each snap multiplies the points.
    Gap shotShoot through a gap in the chain, then make a match, for a big bonus.
    CoinsShoot the spinning ₦ coins for ₦1,000 each.
    Power-upsClear a glowing orb: Bomb, Slow-mo, Reverse, Laser sight, or a Wild orb that matches anything.
    BossesEvery match you make sends fire at the boss. Hitting it directly does a little damage too.
